ADDITIONAL INSIGHTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. STALE PLACE_IDs ARE COMMON
   348 of 2,045 Tier 1 rows (17%) had an expired Place_ID that returned
   a 404 from Google. The script recovered new IDs for most via Text Search.
   This suggests the dataset was built over an extended period and older
   entries were never refreshed. Recommend a periodic re-validation cadence.

2. PHASE 2 HAS HIGHER CORRECTION RATE
   Phase 2 (unchecked rows) had a 54.4% auto-correction/fill rate vs
   14.5% for Phase 1. This is expected — Phase 1 entries had already been
   operator-reviewed; Phase 2 entries are newer and less curated.

3. CITY FIELD WAS THE MOST COMMON CORRECTION
   91 rows (mostly Phase 2) had the city field corrected against Google's
   canonical locality value. Common pattern: a broader region name (e.g.,
   "Greater London", "New York Metro") used instead of the specific city.

4. 90 ROWS NEED MANUAL PLACE_ID LOOKUP
   These rows returned no Google results and have no Suggested_Correction.
   Most likely causes: highly specific/private venues not in Google Maps
   (private estates, yacht clubs, invitation-only spaces), or entry names
   too generic for a confident text search match.

5. CROSS-PHASE DUPLICATES ARE LIKELY ENTRY ERRORS
   The 15 cross-phase duplicate Place_IDs are the highest-priority review
   items in output_flagged.csv. In most cases the Phase 2 row is either
   a duplicate of an existing Phase 1 entry or should be consolidated.
   Filter output_flagged.csv by "DUPLICATE_PLACE_ID" to review these first.

6. NON-PHYSICAL ENTRIES (70 TOTAL)
   57 Phase 1 + 13 Phase 2 rows have no Place_ID, city, or address.
   These are experiences without a fixed physical location (charters,
   custom itineraries, private events). No action needed on these —
   they cannot and should not have a Place_ID assigned.
