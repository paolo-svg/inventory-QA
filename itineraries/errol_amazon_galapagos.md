**Aqua Nera, 4-Night High Water Itinerary**

**Day 1, Tuesday — Iquitos, Nauta, Embarkation**
- Arrive on a noon flight, receive a warm welcome at Iquitos International Airport by the crew
- Private transfer to downtown Iquitos for a city tour and welcome lunch
- Continue to Nauta port to board Aqua Nera
- Settle into your suite, introduction from naturalist guides, routine safety drill
- Gather at the restaurant for the first of many Amazonian cuisine experiences created by Aqua Nera's consulting chef, Pedro Miguel Schiaffino

**Embarkation time:** 5:30pm
**Inclusions:** Dinner
**Recommended flight:** LATAM, LA2019 (LIM to IQT)

- Early morning birdwatching session along the Marañon River
- Breakfast in the middle of the jungle

- Look for the legendary pink river dolphins
- Board skiffs to cruise along the Yanayquillo River for a dusk excursion, searching for nocturnal animals

- Lunch onboard with human history briefing
- Visit Post Office Bay; Zodiac or paddleboard at Baroness Point
- Espresso Martini on sundeck; evening briefing and dinner
